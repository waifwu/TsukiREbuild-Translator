Click a line to see its page.

Laid out by the game's own text box and styles (the fit page): the rows a page takes (12 fit the screen), each choice's width against the widest English one, and in Ciel-sensei's corner whether every letter stays on its balloon. Pictures are not drawn.

Export

Each line with its number, scene, status and note, beside its source line and the English. Italics travel as <i>…</i> (in XLIFF as tags), and come back as they were.

Import

Lines are laid on their line numbers (by their source text where a file has none). Check says what would be placed, kept and left, and writes nothing. A deepLuna project folder is imported with web-dev/tools/exchange.py.

Glossary

A term is found in a line's Japanese or English (or any other column's language: add its code), and its translation is checked for in the line (or another of its forms, "also", for languages that inflect). Kept in the translations folder as glossary.json.
